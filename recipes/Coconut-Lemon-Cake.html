Aussie Lemon Coconut Slice Cake

I'm sure this style of cake isn't exclusively Australian, but I call it Australian because it's a copycat of lemon cake slices I grew up seeing in cafes and at school bake sales. These days you'll even find packaged versions in grocery stores!

It's a thin lemon butter cake cut into handheld pieces and thickly iced with a glaze that crusts on the surface, making it practical for wraping, stackinf and transporting without frosting smearing everywhere. Sometimes it has coconut in it, sometimes it doesn't. I like it with so mine does!

Ingredients in Aussie lemon Coconut Slice Cake

Here's what you'll need to make this.

FOR THE CAKE PART

  • 1 very big lemon or 2 regular lemons - We need 1 tablespoon of zest and 2 tablespoons of juice for the cake, and 7 - 8 tablespoons for the icing. Fun fact: lemon flavour is in the zest. The juice just makes things tangy.
  • Flour - Just plain / all-purpose flour , not self-raising flour(wrong amount of baking powder built in for this cake)
  • Desiccated coconut - This is the finely shredded type that is like panko breadcrumb size, called shredded coconut or finely shredded coconut in the US. Not shavings or flakes(too large), not coconut powder. Make sure it's unsweetened (standard in Australia).

    • Note: You can leave the coconut out if you prefer, and make a lemon cake. That's actually how this recipe started out, but then I added coconut and like it even more.

  • Baking powder -This is what makes the cake rise. We only need 1 teaspoon. Don't substitute with the baking soda. the cake gets a bit of a hump in the middle(we want flat for this slice-cake)
  • Unsalted butter - Softened to room temperature so it can be creamed.
  • Large eggs - eggs around ~50 - 55g/20oz each.
  • Milk - Just regular milk is the liquid for this cake!
  • Salt - Just a tiny amount. to bring out the flavours in everything else.

How to Make an Aussie Lemon Coconut Slice Cake

  • Mix the dry ingredients in a medium bowl (flour, coconut, baking powder and salt)
  • Use a handheld beater and add butter and sugear and beat them for 2 min until light and fluffy.
  • Beat in the eggs one at a time untill mix in, then the lemon juice, zest and vanilla
  • Add 1/3 of the flour mixture nd beat on low speed just until the flour is mixed in, then stop. Add half the milk, beat on low just until incorporated.
  • Bake for 30 min at 180°C/350°F or until a toothpic inserted into the center comes out clean
  • Cool in the pan for 5 min, then turn out onto a rack and cool it upside down to flatten for icing.
  • Put a thick glaze of icing on the flat surface almost like buttercream so it sits on the cake than running down the sides.
